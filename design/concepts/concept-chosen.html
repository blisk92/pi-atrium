Feasibility Study — Cricket Club Q3 Shoot

Prepared by Feasibility · 2026-06-11

Access

Main gate only, 8am–6pm. East entrance locked after 4pm. Guest pass required for crew (apply 3 working days ahead via venue coordinator).

Power

Outdoor outlets are 30m+ from the primary shooting positions on the south lawn. Plan for battery power OR a 50m extension lead from the pavilion.

Sound

Adjacent tennis courts are active until 7pm. Expect ambient noise (rackets, balls) during late-afternoon takes. Consider lavalier mics or post-take dialogue replacement.

Risks

  • Weather: Open location, no cover. Mid-June sun is harsh after 11am — schedule critical takes before 10am or after 4pm.
  • Backlight: East-facing windows mean morning shots will be backlit. Plan for reflectors or repositioning.
  • Foot traffic: Saturday matches draw 50–80 spectators. Avoid weekend shoots or budget for crowd control.

Style Brief — GETFMM Q3

Compiled by Concierge from the client's creative direction.

Tone

Dynamic. Walk-and-shoot. Fluid motion. The viewer should feel like they're moving through the location, not watching a static documentary.

Pacing

  • Open with a slow establishing shot (3–5s)
  • Transition to fluid gimbal work for the body
  • End with a wide pull-back or drone lift

Color

Natural, slightly desaturated. Avoid heavy color grading. Let the location's natural light do the work.
